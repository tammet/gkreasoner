# O13: agreement with the tool

This part holds the comparison of gk 1.3.2 with the tool. The exact method and the default answer every
question; the tool computes the same question by the definition of the semantics, exactly or by sampling. The
questions come from four parts: two generated sets of small inputs (`sets` and `keychk`), the collection of part
O2 (`record`) and the benchmark families of part O3 (`families`). The part also holds two further checks: gk's two
evaluators on the tool's own lists of applications, and the atoms on which the general method misses evidence that
the tool finds.

## Where the inputs come from

- `sets`: small inputs generated by our scripts for comparison with the tool, in 22 sets (`questions_sets.csv`
  names the set of each). Every atom of an input is asked as its own question.
- `keychk`: small random inputs generated for the check of the exact method; every atom is asked, some also as
  their negation.
- `record` and `families`: the inputs of parts O2 and O3, with the answers that those parts list.
- The evaluator check and the missed evidence: generated inputs of six sets (`evaluator.csv`); the atoms of two
  of the sets are asked one at a time (`questions_missed_evidence.csv`).
- The five kinds of missed evidence: the small inputs `inputs/kinds/`, one per kind.
- The further questions: new inputs of the same generators, packed in `inputs/new.tar.xz` (listing
  `inputs/new.files.tsv.gz`): `new/n1a`, at the stored settings, `new/n1b`, the larger inputs, and
  `new/pairs`, the pairs with function terms.

The inputs of the generated sets are packed: `inputs/sets.tar.xz`, `inputs/keychk.tar.xz` and
`inputs/evaluator.tar.xz`, each with a listing `*.files.tsv`. A question file is an input with its question
replaced by one atom. The folder does not store the question files; `tools/make_questions.py` writes them into
`questions/` from the inputs and the lists of questions:

```sh
cd O13_conformance && for a in inputs/*.tar.xz; do tar -xJf $a -C inputs; done && cd ..
python3 tools/make_questions.py
```

It writes every question file of the runs. With `--check FOLDER` it writes nothing and compares the files that it
would write with the question files of an earlier run in FOLDER.

## Rerun

The whole part reruns more than a hundred thousand runs; the check of the generated sets alone takes about an
hour with eight processes at a time:

```sh
python3 check_gk.py --part O13_conformance --gk PATH/TO/gk --mcref PATH/TO/mcref.py --jobs 8
```

It needs the question files (above). The runs and values that it compares:

<!-- table check -->
| gk runs | tool runs | runs with a recorded value | values compared | of these, on runs that did not end by themselves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 350,836 | 58,677 | 331,346 | 332,100 | 3,779 |
<!-- end table -->

The evaluator check runs the tool in its own process and gk's evaluators on the tool's lists of applications:

```sh
cd O13_conformance && MCREF_DIR=PATH/TO/mcref_folder MCREF_GK=PATH/TO/gk python3 tools/evaluator_check.py out.json @evaluator_list_0.txt
```

`$GK` stands for the gk binary and `$MCREF` for the tool's `mcref.py`. Every command of `runs.tsv` runs
in the folder that its column `cwd` names, relative to this folder's parent (`paper2026/`).

### The general method by time limit

The questions of the missed-evidence check also ran under the general method at the time limits of 1 s and 10 s
(`-seconds 1` and `-seconds 10`), with the evaluator's dump (`-dwdump`). "Equal" means within 1e-6 of the tool's
exact value. A sign is unfinished work that the run reports: a stopped, queued or unrunnable task, a pending
step, a step that the dump does not represent, an interrupted evaluation, or an item of the dump's list of
unfinished work. A note of an approximation in the dump is no sign.

<!-- table by_time -->
| time limit | answer | reported unfinished work | reported none | all |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 s | equal to the tool | 982 | 3,810 | 4,792 |
| 1 s | differs from the tool | 32 | 7 | 39 |
| 10 s | equal to the tool | 880 | 3,919 | 4,799 |
| 10 s | differs from the tool | 23 | 9 | 32 |
<!-- end table -->

`general_by_time.csv` has one row per question and time limit: the limit in seconds (`seconds`), gk's `value`,
the tool's value (`target`), the `class`, and the sign (`unfinished_work`) with its two parts
(`stopped_or_queued_search`, `other_unfinished_work`). The column `approximation_note` says whether the dump lists
a note of an approximation.

### Further questions on new inputs

generated after the build was fixed. The generators of this part's generated inputs wrote new inputs with new

## Where the paper's numbers are

- The agreement sentence (Section 7.4): the table "the exact method against the tool", from `results.csv.gz`,
  rows with `method` exact, column `class`, by `part`.
- The causes of the answers that are not complete: the same rows, column `first_limit`.
- The evaluators on the tool's applications: `evaluator.csv`, columns `atoms`, `general_differ`, `exact_differ`.
- The evidence that the search does not collect: `missed_evidence.csv`, columns `beyond_.002` and
  `beyond_1e-6`; the five kinds: `kinds.csv` and the rows of `part` kinds.
- The general method by time limit: `general_by_time.csv`, columns `seconds`, `class` and
  `unfinished_work`.
- The further questions (Section 7.4): the rows of `part` new and new_larger, columns `complete` and `class`;
  the pairs with function terms: the rows of `part` pairs.

`results.csv` has one row per run and answer. The columns are described on the first page of the
folder ([`../README.md`](../README.md), section "The tables of a part").
